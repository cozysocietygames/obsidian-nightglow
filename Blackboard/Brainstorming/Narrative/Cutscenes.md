## Sample Scene 1 - Being Utterly Defeated By Pepper
Trigger: After first battle with Pepper
Setting: Out/in of the first Gym.

\[...]
_Pepper comes out of the gym, emotes with an exclamation point, walks straight to you and starts a battle._ [^1]

\[battle]

Pepper: Oh, so sorry. I thought you were a seasoned trainer. You were giving off that aura, so I didn't want to go easy on you.

_Gym Leader comes out of the gym_. [^1]

Gym Leader: You have a lot to learn, Pepper. Differentiating potential from experience takes time.
Gym Leader *(to you)*: So I guess you're a challenger? Follow me.

[^1]: Animations look like this: Narration first, and it corresponds to an animation once the animations are available.
