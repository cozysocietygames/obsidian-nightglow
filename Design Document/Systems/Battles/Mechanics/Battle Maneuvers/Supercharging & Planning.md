# Supercharging & Planning (Cool & Beauty)

Pokemon can use [SP](SP.md) to supercharge their main move, applying their Cool and Beauty (in conjunction with their Atk and Sp. Atk) upon calculating the damage, but decreasing the accuracy to `p%` of its value.

- `p` is the Planning factor.
- On the first round, `p` is a low number, like `30%`.
- `p` increases by an amount each round, tentatively `10%`.
