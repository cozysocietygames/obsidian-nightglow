# Traits
I was considering a system in which Pokemon have Traits that are permanent in nature. A Trait is composed of a Trait Name and at least one Trait Effect. 

Two ways to model it:
1. Trait Effects define the Trait and a Trait Name is just a label for it
> [!example] Trait Effects define the Trait
> 
> We conceptualize a certain Trait gives a Pokemon an extra  [[On Officers#Lookouts|Lookout Charge]]. Its name will thus be called `Great Lookout`.  The `Great Lookout` Trait will thus always refer to the trait that gives an extra Lookout Charge.
2. Trait Name defines  the Trait and Trait Effects are ways that the Trait manifest
> [!example] Trait Name defines the Trait
> 
> We conceptualize a Trait called `Great Lookout`. We decide to attach a Trait Effect that gives them an extra Lookout Charge. The trait will always be called `Great Lookout` but its manifestation is advertised to change based on what Trait Effects are attached to it.
