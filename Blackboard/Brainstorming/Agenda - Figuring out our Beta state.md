### Abstraction of Space

![[Pasted image 20260905113540.png|227]]
#### Off-camera Spaces
- Implied physical spaces whose exact layout is left up to the player's imagination. 
- Expansion of the space is as simple as saying there is more usable space now.
#### On-camera Spaces
- Visually represented physical spaces which can usually be populated by entities that have some notion of size and position. 
- Expansion of the location itself is more constrained because it must be visually represented, such as by a new part of a map.

#### Pocket Dimension
- A Pocket Dimension is an On-camera space whose map represents the interior of some container on an outer dimension. It can usually be expanded freely without much alteration of its appearance on the outer dimension. 

- Pocket Dimensions occupy a constant *maximum* dedicated exterior space even when upgraded, with only minor aesthetic illusions for immersion.

### Which On-camera Spaces do we need in Beta state?

> [!warning] The Old Plan: Ports and Surrounding Maps
> The old plan was that ports/docks were necessary because that would be where players start the game, plus the maps surrounding them so the players get to explore around them.
> 
> >[!warning] Problem
> >
> >Progress is highly gated and thus wild pokemon they encounter will be severely limited due to the geographic constraints. Developer's time constraints are dictating the flow and pacing of the game as opposed to an intentional design of pacing. 
> >
> >

> [!question] Town and City Maps first and introduce Explorable Maps as needed?
> 
> What if we don't prioritize walking as a method of travel over long distances?

Prioritizing **Town and City Maps** as On-camera spaces allows the Government to be fully functional earlier on, as opposed to only having one or two working cities for a long time. We can station Alakazams at Town and City Centers to allow fast travel between them.

We can introduce several key **Wilderness Locations** that players can explore early on by Alakazamming into and out of them. 

Fiction explanation could be that explorable areas are highly regulated as mandated by the national government due to safety concerns.

By prioritizing Alakazam travel, we allow ourselves the freedom to develop maps as needed without worrying much about the exact method of how the player will access them. 

Prioritize Wilderness Locations that provide the fundamental resources needed for crafting and construction gameplay, as well as offer a sufficient selection of wild Pokemon.

1. Town and City Maps
	- Necessary if we want space management to be part of Government's building management gameplay.
2. Route Maps
	- Necessary if we need to visually represent the player walking to different towns.
3. Tent Map
	- Necessary if we want the player to have a base whose visual layout they can customize.
4. Dungeon Maps
	- Necessary for explorable pocket dimensions.
