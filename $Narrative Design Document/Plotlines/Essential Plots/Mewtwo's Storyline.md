
- Sometime in the past, Mewtwo meets Arceus, and understands that there are powers far greater than him.
    
- Sometime in the past as well, Mewtwo becomes friends with Pepper’s parents and becomes close to their family, children and all.
    
- When the catastrophe strikes, Mewtwo begins to question whether coexistence is possible between humans and Pokemon, given that plenty of legendary Pokemon are capable of so much destruction. Other legendaries helped deal with the consequences of the catastrophe, but others felt like the humans brought it upon themselves for trying to awaken the legendaries.
    
- Mewtwo had a falling out with the parents long ago because the parents wanted to clone legendaries and take control of them themselves.
    

- They try to win back his friendship by offering him control over the clones.
    
- They have two devices: one tailored for Velvet’s parents, and one tailored for Mewtwo. They promise Mewtwo that if he takes the offer, they will destroy their device. Of course, if Mewtwo refuses, then they’d have no choice but to destroy Mewtwo’s device and to take it upon themselves to control them (implying Mewtwo would be out of the picture)… But they know that humans holding control over clones has a bad history, which is why they wanted to offer the proposal to Mewtwo.
    

- They do not inform Mewtwo that the device tailored for Mewtwo contains something that would allow them to control him in the worst case. As a deterrent for Mew, and to keep him in check as well if he loses his way.
    
- But Mewtwo finds out anyway.
    

- They try to explain themselves once more, arguing that they must do it as justice for losing Pepper’s sister and so that others will not have to experience the same thing.
    
- BV expresses reassurance that Mewtwo is nothing like Giovanni. If anything, they can rest assured that Mewtwo would never use the clones for selfish gain. 
    
- Mewtwo experiences a dilemma:
    

- On one hand, he believes that he is indeed capable; he has grown. He believes that if he gains control over the clones, he will not misuse them or mistreat them like Giovanni and his team did to him. (Velvet-y)
    
- On the other hand, he is afraid that he might become corrupt; he might get lost in his principles if they become too extreme. He has made mistakes before, after all. (Sand-y)
    
- On another hand, he considers perhaps it is best not to clone the Pokemon in the first place, because he is worried at the notion of adding more legendary beings into the picture, because there is already enough destructive power in the world. (UPL-y)
    

- On speaking terms once more, Mewtwo helps Pepper’s mom with a personal favor: erasing Pepper’s memory. Mewtwo agrees, because he also believes that Pepper should have the choice not to follow Black Velvet.
    

- Pepper’s memory is returned later on; she gets bits and pieces from interacting with her Cherrim. She remembers bits and pieces of her sister. Finally, she asks Sand’s Xatu to look into her past. Xatu talks about her sister, she is in denial about being part of Black Velvet because her connection with Red and the rest of the UPL feels real. Throughout the journey, until the decision, she begins to recall some memories and to start thinking about the consequences of it, and what path she should follow from here on out. When she meets Velvet Elite 1 and the Champion with you by her side, she confirms it with Velvet Elite 1. Which is why she is forced to make a decision then as well about which side she will ultimately take.
    

- Near the end of the game, Mewtwo reminds the player of the consequences of cloning Arceus. Because Mewtwo himself could have caused plenty of destruction if he was left to his own devices. 
    

- The player/Pepper asks Mewtwo what stopped him?
    
- Mewtwo says it is because he learned who he is, and he learned that he has a place for himself in this world.
    
- The player/Pepper asks Mewtwo if he can teach the same things to the other cloned legendaries if the cloned legendaries ever come into being.
    
- Mewtwo says if it has to come to that, then of course he will try. He knows the pain of not knowing where you belong. He knows the pain of being so different from the rest of the world—the pain of being so different that you don’t even know where to begin understanding who you are.
    
- The player/Pepper is convinced that Mewtwo didn’t turn out so bad, and so they know they can count on Mewtwo for what is to come.
