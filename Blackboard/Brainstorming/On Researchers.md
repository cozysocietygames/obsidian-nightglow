> [!warning] Old Design
> 
> A Player could hire a Researcher to increase the stats of their Pokemon. There was an upper cap.


> [!warning] Old Brainstormed Concept - Three-Letter Codes
> A three-letter string is assigned to each Pokemon. ABC would correspond to a solution of like 60%A 30%B 10%C.  Researchers test various solutions and get outputs. e.g. If they test AFC it would be a 70% Match. Researchers could share their findings to the public, and other Researchers can use their research as a basis for how to proceed.
> 
> The biggest problem in this that we could see at the time was that once they've found all the combinations, there is no longer any sense of discovery, thus their Pokemon Research gameplay becomes stale.
