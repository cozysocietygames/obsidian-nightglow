# Chapter ? — Summit
## Act 3. 
> [!note] [[Main Storyline#Act 3. Setting Ashore|Act 3]]
> [[1 - Chapter 1 Storyboard.canvas|See Storyboard]]
> 
_The legendary Pokemon awakens, and you and Pepper must each make a decision. Whether it be to side with the UPL or to side with Black Velvet, you make your decision known, and their representatives continue their battle in order to buy you time to destroy or activate the cloning machine._
>^cSa3

Champion4 and BVE4 are still battling each other, but Blue and Green are battling Pepper’s parents. The proud leaders of Velvet call in doctors to heal their Pokemon, and just when they think they can proceed, Pepper decisively steps in and stops them. You arrive just as she wins, and the parents call in the doctors to heal their Pokemon in one last desperate attempt to fight, and you defeat them.

While you were battling, Pepper swayed the doctors to her personal cause, restoring her Pokemon to full health. For a moment you think she is now on your side, but she battles you anyway, still intent on cloning the Pokemon. But ultimately, she loses to you.

She explains everything she wanted to do. It is ultimately the humans in general that Arceus distrusts; you were an exception because you were on their side all the way.  After hearing that Pepper didn’t plan on controlling the clones, Arceus asks you once more if you would stand by them no matter what. Arceus decides to entrust their DNA to Pepper (but only to Pepper, not BV), knowing that they can always count on you if something goes wrong.

Pepper’s parents defeat Blue and Green. You arrive just in time to reveal that you are turning against them. You defeat them and acquire the key to the lab of cloned Pokemon. They call in doctors to heal their Pokemon and start to chase after you, but Pepper arrives and keeps them occupied with a battle. Meanwhile, Arceus gets a burst of strength and you must restrain him; in the process, he finds out that you are not like Pepper’s parents—that you are not so ambitious as to dream of monopolizing control over the Velvet clones. As you weaken Arceus, Pepper finally defeats her mom and dad, and she sways the doctors to her side; she is dead set on taking you down. You defeat Pepper in order to accomplish your personal goals as you’ve discussed with Mewtwo.

When you successfully clone Arceus, he expresses some respect for your determination and your philosophy, but he also says outright that you are not his friend. In the worst case, he knows he can count on Pepper’s best efforts to save the world; he has to, because now there is twice more power around than there used to be. And no one to control it.
