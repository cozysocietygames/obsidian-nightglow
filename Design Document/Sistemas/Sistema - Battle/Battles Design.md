# Design Philosophy

> [!important] Design Philosophy
> 
> *In the spirit of the anime, Pokemon should be able to express their potential beyond their primary stats and 4 moves, to a controlled degree so that trainer tactics still matter and do not feel as if they are dominated by wild cards.*
> 
> #designphilosophy
