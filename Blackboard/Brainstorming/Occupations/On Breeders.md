# Traits
I was considering a system in which Pokemon have Traits that are permanent in nature. A Trait is composed of a Trait Name and at least one Trait Effect. 

We get more flexibility if we treat a Trait as a composition of its Trait Effects.

Say we conceptualize a `Great Lookout`  trait. It does nothing on its own except serve as a label that describes what kind of effects a Player might expect. For a certain version, we could define the trait to have the `Extra Lookout Charge` effect, which grants them an extra [[On Officers#Lookouts|Lookout Charge]]. In a future update, we might redefine the `Great Lookout` trait to have a different effect instead, for example `Prolonged Protection` which grants the player a Protected status for a longer duration when a Lookout Charge is consumed. In this way, we can refine the effects of traits for the sake of rebalancing without having to remove the traits from the Pokemon.
