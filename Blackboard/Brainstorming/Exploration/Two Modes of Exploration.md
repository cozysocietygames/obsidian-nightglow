# Overview
In the Overview mode, you do not control a character. Instead, you control the map camera, and you can pan it to anywhere on the map.

Clicking a Building allows you to interact with it: see its details, or even enter it.

You can see Wild Pokemon roaming about. If you click on one, you can interact with it:
- record it as seen in your Pokedex
- observe it
	- to glean some details about that specific Pokemon
	- to gain Familiarity towards that species
- attempt to capture it without battling
- battle it

Clicking on an empty spot on spawnable ground allows you to spawn there and switch to Exploration mode.
# Exploration
In the Exploration mode, you control your player character and you can see them walking on the map.

Your character must be on Exploration mode to unlock new areas.

Just like in Overview mode, you can see Wild Pokemon roaming about. If you click on one, you can interact with it in the same ways.
