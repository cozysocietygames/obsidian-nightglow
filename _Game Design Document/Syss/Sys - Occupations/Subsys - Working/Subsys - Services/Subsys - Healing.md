%%  How the system works, in technical language, as specific as possible. %%
Healing is a [[Subsys - Services|service]] available to [[Subsys - Doctor|doctors]]. Performing this service restores HP to maximum and clears any Non-Volatile Conditions of a team of Pokemon of the [[Consumer]]. By default, the subset is their entire Active Team of up to 6 Pokemon.
