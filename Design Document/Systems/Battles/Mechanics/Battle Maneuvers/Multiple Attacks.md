# Multiple Attacks (Cute & Speed)

In a single round, apart from the main move used by the Pokemon, a Pokemon can perform several extra generic attacks out of the sheer perseverance of someone faster.

- Each extra attack is a generic attack; not a Pokemon move.
- Each extra attack deals True Damage.
- Each extra attack costs a certain amount of [SP](SP.md).
- The number of extra attacks that can be done depends on the ratio of your stats to your opponent’s stats.
    - The stats used are a combination of Primary Stats and Appeal Stats.
