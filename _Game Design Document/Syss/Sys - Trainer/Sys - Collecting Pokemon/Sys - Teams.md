## Team
The Main Team can hold a maximum of 6 Pokemon and a minimum of 1 Pokemon. It is implied to be physically present with the player at all times. This team is what the Trainer uses for battling and exploring the overworld.

## Miniteams #discuss 
Apart from their Team, a player may assemble Miniteams. Miniteams can hold a maximum of 3 Pokemon, and unlike the main team, do not need to be with their trainer at all times. There is a maximum number of miniteams that can be assembled. In fact, the main purpose of miniteams is to be dispatched to certain posts. The posts they may be dispatched to are listed below:
### Tentkeeping Post
A miniteam may be assembled for their Tent. This miniteam guards the Tent and can assist with crafting.
### Gathering 
A miniteam may be assembled for the purpose of gathering Resources.
### Occupation Assistance
A miniteam may be assembled for Occupation-related activities. The different varieties are listed below:
#### Breeder: Nurturing Miniteam

#### Criminal: Heist Miniteam

#### Doctor: Nursing Miniteam

#### Officer: Lookout Miniteam

#### Researcher: Research Miniteam

#### Educator: Teaching Miniteam
