Battle Maneuvers are alternative options that a Player may choose to command their Pokemon to do.

### List of Battle Maneuvers

1. [[Last Stand]] (HP & Intimacy)
2. [[Multiple Attacks]] (Spd & Cute)
3. [[Supercharging & Planning]] (Atk, Sp. Atk & Cool, Beauty)
4. [[Defensive Maneuver]] (Def, Sp. Def & Tough, Smart)
