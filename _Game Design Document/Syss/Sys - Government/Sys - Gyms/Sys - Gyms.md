## Gym Minions
Below the Gym Leader, there are minions of lower tiers, with the lowest being `IV` and the highest—right below the gym leader in the hierarchy—being `I`.

A player may challenge a Gym Minion of tier `N` if they have won and challenged a Gym Minion of a tier directly below it, if any, in the recent past (Timespan unspecified). If they win, their Authority gets upgraded to match that of the Gym Minion they just beat. Whether or not they automatically replace them should be pondered upon more.
