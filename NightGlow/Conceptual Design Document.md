# Pokemon NightGlow
(legacy name)

## Genre
Pokemon-themed browser-based Sandbox MMO, City/Community Builder, Political/Economic Sim

### Inspirations
1. Pokemon - Creature-Collector adventure JRPG.
2. Pokemon Crater - browser-based Pokemon fangame.
3. Wonderland Online - MMORPG where you can capture pets, and fight alongside them. It features a unique movable player-housing system supported by a rich crafting system.
4. Anno1777 - Browser-based Political/Economic Strategy Sim.
## Platform & Devices
Web browsers on PC and Mobile, possibly Mobile App in the future
