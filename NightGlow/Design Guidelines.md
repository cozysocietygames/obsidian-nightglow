# Core Philosophy

> [!important] Core Philosophy
>Your choices shape the world.
# Guiding Principles

> [!important] Core Guiding Principle
>  _We want to create a dynamic world that responds to user activity to emulate a living, breathing society that makes the player want to shape it._
> 

> [!tip] Mood of the Game
> The mood on the collective scale is harsh survival against disasters as buildings are demolished by storms and they find they have to rebuild again.
> 
> The mood on the individual scale will depend on how well the government tends to individuals' needs, but it is always supplemented with the joy of caring for your favorite Pokemon
