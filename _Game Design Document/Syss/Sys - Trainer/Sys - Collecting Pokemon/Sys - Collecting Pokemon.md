> 
> _Friendship first, before mastery._
> 
> _Trainers should be able to express their potential through BOTH friendship and mastery.  Although a common catchphrase of Pokemon is "Gotta catch 'em all," we intend to align more with the principles of the anime instead, in which Ash repeatedly lets Pokemon go when necessary._

## Familiarity
The Trainer has a `Familiarity` value for each species of Pokemon. Higher values of familiarity gives several benefits:
1. Higher modified catch rate
2. Slower Amity Decay for Pokemon of that species #discuss 
3. Occupation-related benefits: #discuss 
	1. Unspecified Benefit for Educators when teaching about that Pokemon?
	2. Unspecified Benefit for Doctors when treating that Pokemon?
	3. Unspecified Benefit for Breeders when training that Pokemon?
	4. Unspecified Benefit for Criminals when stealing that Pokemon? or when using that Pokemon in their Heist Team?
	5. Unspecified Benefit for Officers when using that Pokemon as a Lookout? or when trying to apprehend such a Pokemon on a Heist Team?
