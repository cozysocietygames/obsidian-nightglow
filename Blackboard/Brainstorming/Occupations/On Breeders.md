Two ways to model it:
1. Trait Effects define the Trait and a Trait Name is just a label for it
> [!example] Trait Effects define the Trait
> 
> A certain Trait gives a Pokemon a better chance 
1. Trait Name defines  the Trait and Trait Effects are ways that the Trait manifest
